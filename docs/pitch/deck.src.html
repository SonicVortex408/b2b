The problem

Campus space is harder to use than it should be

Rooms exist; finding, booking and trusting them is the hard part.

Finding is fragmented

Nobody can see which of 37 classrooms, labs and halls is free right now. Answers live in group chats and HOD cabins.

Booking is manual

Requests wait in inboxes with no deadline, and there is no record of who asked first or why a request was refused.

Clashes surface at the door

Two groups booked for one room only find out when both arrive. Exams collide with club events.

Capacity stays hidden

Rooms booked by people who never show up stay locked all afternoon while demand piles up elsewhere.

The guarantee

A double booking cannot be written

Postgres itself refuses overlapping holds. App logic is never the only defence, and pending requests hold the slot too.

constraint bookings_no_overlap
  exclude using gist (
    resource_id with =,
    tstzrange(starts_at, ends_at) with &&
  ) where (status in (
    'confirmed', 'pending_approval', 'checked_in'))

Every write goes through one transaction, apply_engine_changes(). A lost race raises 23P01; the server reloads and lets the engine decide again.

Ghost bookings

Booked but empty? Released in 10 minutes

Each door shows a signed QR that changes every 60 seconds. No scan within 10 minutes of start, and the room goes back to the campus.

✓

Scan to check in

HS256 token, per-room secret, 60 s lifetime. Expired, wrong-room or tampered codes are refused.

↺

No-show releases the slot

The waitlist is promoted and reliability points drop by 10.

◎

Leave early, hand it on

“Room free now” goes to everyone; first tap claims it (+5).
